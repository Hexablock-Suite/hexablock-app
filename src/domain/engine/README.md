Generic engine: the game loop. It holds the board, the coordinates and the geometry families, the pieces and the piece sources, the turn, the phases, the rule and scoring contracts, the history, the timer, `GameFactory`, `IVariantFactory` and `LevelVariant`, which turns one level into a variant factory (#407). It may import only `src/domain/`, never `src/domain/variants/`, and never React, React Native or Expo. It imports the leaves of `src/domain/shared/`. Its imports of `src/domain/progress/` are types: `IProgressModel` in `factory/variant-factory.ts`, because `IVariantFactory.createProgressModel()` gives the progress model of a game, `LevelDefinition` in `factory/level-variant.ts`, because a `LevelVariant` builds the variant factory of one level definition, and `LevelId` in `turn/game-engine.ts` and `factory/game-factory.ts` (decisions 31, 59 and 64 of `docs/architecture/classes.md`).

## Subfolders

The files sit in subfolders by game concern (#499, decision 64). A subfolder imports only the subfolders of its row, so no two subfolders import each other through any path. The rule `engine-folders-no-cycle` of `.dependency-cruiser.cjs` refuses a cycle between two subfolders, and `test/gates/architecture-gate.test.ts` tests it on a fixture with a cycle.

| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| `turn/`     | `GameEngine`, the phases, `Resolution`, the rule and scoring contracts and their decorators, the timer and the clock | `board/`, `events/`, `history/` and the root          | `shared/`, `progress/` (`LevelId`)      |
| `board/`    | `Board`, `BoardBuilder`, `BoardState`, `Piece`, `BoardId` and `GameStateId`                                          | `geometry/`, `events/` and the root                   | `shared/`                               |
| `geometry/` | The topologies, the geometry families, the shape Composite and the values of a coordinate                            | Nothing                                               | `shared/`                               |
| `history/`  | `MoveCommand`, `MoveHistory`, `RedoableHistory`, the undo capabilities and `UndoLimit`                               | Nothing                                               | Nothing                                 |
| `events/`   | `GameEvent`, `GameEnded` and `Outcome`                                                                               | The root                                              | `shared/`                               |
| `factory/`  | `GameFactory`, `IVariantFactory`, `LevelVariant` and `MatchIdGenerator`                                              | `turn/`, `board/`, `history/`, `random/` and the root | `shared/`, `progress/` (types)          |

| Root        | `MatchId`, `Color` and `Cloneable`                                                                                   | Nothing                                               | `shared/`                               |
