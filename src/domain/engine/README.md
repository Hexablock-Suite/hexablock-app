Generic engine: the game loop. It holds the board, the coordinates and the geometry families, the pieces and the piece sources, the turn, the phases, the rule and scoring contracts, the history, the timer, `GameFactory`, `IVariantFactory` and `LevelVariant`, which turns one level into a variant factory (#407). It may import only `src/domain/`, never `src/domain/variants/`, and never React, React Native or Expo. It imports the leaves of `src/domain/shared/`. Its imports of `src/domain/progress/` are two types: `IProgressModel` in `variant-factory.ts`, because `IVariantFactory.createProgressModel()` gives the progress model of a game, and `LevelDefinition` in `level-variant.ts`, because a `LevelVariant` builds the variant factory of one level definition (decisions 31, 59 and 64 of `docs/architecture/classes.md`).
