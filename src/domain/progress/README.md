Progress of the player and the level catalog. It holds `GameProgress`, whose `mergeWith()` merges another progress of its game by the rule of DS-12 (#46), the progress models (`IProgressModel`, `GameResult`, `ProgressRecord`, `HighScoreProgress` and `LevelProgress` with the star rules `IStarsByMoves` and `IStarsByMovesAndTime`), `LevelStatus`, `LevelDefinition`, `ILevelBuilder`, `fieldsOf()` for the objects of a level file, `LevelId`, `Stars`, and `starsOf()` with `IStarThreshold`, the one star count of Fit and Sort (#424). It imports only `src/domain/shared/` (rule `progress-imports-only-shared`), and never React, React Native or Expo. The engine names `IProgressModel` in `IVariantFactory.createProgressModel()`, so each variant factory gives the progress model of its game (decisions 31 and 64 of `docs/architecture/classes.md`).
