Use cases and ports, generic over the variant. It may import only `src/domain/` and other `src/application/` code, and never `src/domain/variants/`.

The aspects of §3.5 live here too. `UseCaseAspect` is the abstract Decorator of `UseCase`, and `LoggingAspect` extends it. The `Logger` port takes a `UseCaseLogEntry`, never a message (decision 37 of the class diagram).

`execute()` never throws (decision 28). An aspect puts its concern in `around()` and calls `super.around()` to run the inner use case. `UseCaseAspect.execute()` turns every exception, from the inner use case or from the aspect's own work, into a failed `Result` with the code `unexpected-failure`, and the `UseCaseError` keeps the exception as its `cause`. `UseCaseError` rejects an empty or blank `code`, and `LoggingAspect` an empty or blank `name`, with a `RangeError`.

`SaveProgress` records a `GameResult` in the `GameProgress` of its game and saves it through the `ProgressRepository` port, the repository of the progress aggregate (#164, decision 57 of the class diagram). It takes the `GameId` of its game and the progress model of the variant in its constructor, and it returns no value. `ProgressRepository` gives back and saves the whole `GameProgress`.

`StartGame` starts a level from its `levelId` (#172, decision 59 of the class diagram). `StartGameRequest` holds an optional `LevelId`, and `StartGame` asks the `GameFactories` port for the `GameFactory` of that level, or of the registered game when the request names no level. A game with no levels starts with no level id. `LevelRepository.levelOf()` gives the definition of one level of a game.

`ListLevels` lists the levels of a game on the chosen figure (#168, decision 58 of the class diagram). Its request holds the `GameId` and the figure as a `TopologyId`. It reads the definitions through the `LevelRepository` port and the progress through `ProgressRepository`, and it returns one `LevelSummary` per level of that figure, in the order of the level files: a `LevelId`, a `LevelStatus` (`completed`, `open` or `locked`) and the `Stars` of a completed level. The first level is open, and a won level opens the next one, also with zero stars (S3-12 in #151). A figure with no level gives an empty list.

`ListAchievements` lists the achievements of the player with their status (#188, decision 60 of the class diagram). Its request has no member. It gives one `AchievementSummary` per achievement, in the order of the list that the composition root gives: the id of the achievement and whether it is completed. An `Achievement` has an id and an optional `AchievementGoal`, the Strategy that decides when it is completed: `LevelsWon` counts the won levels of one game, and `EveryLevelWon` asks for every level of one game. A goal reads the levels through `LevelRepository` and the saved progress through `ProgressRepository`, and a level is won when the progress keeps a result of it, as in `ListLevels`. An achievement with no goal stays in progress.

The failure codes: `start-failed` (`StartGame`), `no-running-game`, `move-rejected` and `move-failed` (`ApplyMove`), `no-undo`, `nothing-to-undo` and `undo-failed` (`UndoMove`, which also returns `no-running-game`), `nothing-to-redo` and `redo-failed` (`RedoMove`, #178, which also returns `no-running-game`), `save-failed` (`SaveProgress`), `list-failed` (`ListLevels` and `ListAchievements`), and `unexpected-failure` (`UseCaseAspect`). The engine records its events, and `ApplyMove` delivers them to the `GameEventPublisher` port after the turn (#233). `ApplyMove` answers success with the saved turn when the publisher throws, and passes a `publish-failed` entry to its `Logger` (decision 29). A `Logger` that throws there does not change that answer.
