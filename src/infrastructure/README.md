Frameworks and drivers (storage, HTTP client, system clock, seeded random source). It may import every inner layer. `app/` holds the Expo Router routes, the outer framework layer.

`composition-root.ts` is the composition root: the only module that imports from every layer (S3-06 in #151, Q9). It builds the drivers `SystemClock`, `SeededRandomSource`, `ConsoleLogger` and `SequentialMatchIds`, the adapters of `src/adapters/` and the game use cases of each game. It builds one `GameEventBus` and gives it to the `ApplyMove` of every game and figure (#362). With the local store of its drivers, it builds one `LocalAchievementCounterRepository`, gives it to `AchievementCounterListener` and subscribes the listener to the bus (#363). A root with no store subscribes no counter listener. Every use case of the root goes through one `decorate(useCase, name)`, which gives it its chain of aspects from the outside in; today the chain holds `LoggingAspect` only (#323). `StartGame` of each game takes one `LevelGameFactories` over the one `BundledLevelRepository` of the root, so it starts a bundled level of Fit or Sort by its id (#172). `game-registry.ts` maps each `GameId` to its variant factory and its screen parts: the state mapper, the cell renderer and the layout of its figure (#170). `registered-games.ts` lists the games, each with the default screen parts until its variant registers its own, and gives Fit and Sort their `LevelVariant`. Hexa Merge has its own parts since #177: `mergeScreen()` gives it `MergeStateMapper`, `MergeCellRenderer` and `MergeControls`. With the local store of its drivers, the root builds one `LocalProgressRepository` and keeps it for every consumer of the saved progress (#323). It builds `ListAchievements` over the same `BundledLevelRepository` and that repository (#188). The store stays optional: a root with no store throws a `RangeError` from `listAchievements()`, and `registered-achievements.ts` lists the achievements with the game of each goal. `app/_layout.tsx` only calls the root through `CompositionRootProvider`, and a route reads it with `useCompositionRoot()`.

`device-key-value-store.ts` holds `DeviceKeyValueStore`, the local storage driver (S3-01 in #151, issue #155). It implements `KeyValueStore` of `src/adapters/` with `@react-native-async-storage/async-storage`, so a value survives a restart of the app. `CompositionRootProvider` builds it and gives it to the root, which builds `LocalProgressRepository` over it for the achievements screen (#188). `jest.setup.js` gives every test of the `app` project of Jest the official mock of the package.

`board/` holds the React Native board components (S3-07 in #151, Q8, issue #165). `BoardView` draws the pairs of a coordinate and a cell view of `GameViewModel` on any figure, with the accessible name of the board. `BoardLayout` is the screen layout of a figure, and `HexLayout`, `SquareLayout` and `TriangleLayout` implement it. `CellRenderer` is the interface of the cell renderer of a variant, and `SpaceCellRenderer` is the default renderer, which tells a free space from an occupied one (#170). `FitCellRenderer` draws a space of Hexa Fit: a socket, or a stone in the color of its piece (#175). `MergeCellRenderer` draws a Merge tile as a stone in the tone of its value with its number (#177). `slideWays()` gives the slides of a Merge board with the way of each one on the screen of the figure. `BoardView` hands a press on a space to the move controls of the game (#175). `fittedCellSize()` gives the widest cell at which a board fits the width of the screen.

`ui/` holds the parts that the screens share (#169): the design tokens of the team prototype in `theme.ts`, the scene of sky and grass, the hex stone, the wood sign and the lip button, and `useHomePresenter()`, which builds `HomePresenter` from the game registry of the root. `GameStage` is the view of the game screen: it keeps `GamePresenter`, starts the game and shows the board, the score and the outcome. `useGameSession()` builds the presenter of one game from the use cases and the screen parts of the root (#170). `MoveControls` is the strategy of the move controls of one game: the stage gives it the board and `play()`, which calls `GamePresenter.onMove()`. `BoardOnlyControls` is the default, and `FitControls` play Hexa Fit with the tray, the rotate button and a press on a space (#175). `MergeControls` play Hexa Merge with one slide button for each way of the figure, and with the prompt that offers to continue when a merge makes the target tile (#177). Since #292 a swipe anywhere on the board plays the way nearest to its angle, and a swipe shorter than 24 px plays nothing. On the web the arrow keys and the keys Q, E, Z and C play the way nearest to the angle of the key: a repeated key plays once, and a key with Ctrl, Meta or Alt plays nothing. `slide-input.ts` holds `swipedWay()` and `useSlideKeys()`, and only this layer reads the platform. The swipe area holds the board only, so a press on a slide button or on the undo button stays a press, and the ring of buttons stays for the screen reader. `app/_layout.tsx` wraps the app in `GestureHandlerRootView`, which the gesture needs. `useAchievements()` builds `AchievementsPresenter` from the root and gives the view model of the achievements screen (#188). Since #179 they also show an undo button below the slide buttons, which takes back the last slide.
