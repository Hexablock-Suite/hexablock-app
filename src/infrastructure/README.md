Frameworks and drivers (storage, HTTP client, system clock, seeded random source). It may import every inner layer. `app/` holds the Expo Router routes, the outer framework layer.

`composition-root.ts` is the composition root: the only module that imports from every layer (S3-06 in #151, Q9). It builds the drivers `SystemClock`, `SeededRandomSource`, `ConsoleLogger` and `SequentialMatchIds`, the adapters of `src/adapters/` and the game use cases of each game inside the logging aspect. `game-registry.ts` maps each `GameId` to its variant factory and its screen parts: the state mapper, the cell renderer and the layout of its figure (#170). `registered-games.ts` lists the games, each with the default screen parts until its variant registers its own. With the local store of its drivers, the root builds `ListAchievements` over `BundledLevelRepository` and `LocalProgressRepository` (#188), and `registered-achievements.ts` lists the achievements with the game of each goal. `app/_layout.tsx` only calls the root through `CompositionRootProvider`, and a route reads it with `useCompositionRoot()`.

`device-key-value-store.ts` holds `DeviceKeyValueStore`, the local storage driver (S3-01 in #151, issue #155). It implements `KeyValueStore` of `src/adapters/` with `@react-native-async-storage/async-storage`, so a value survives a restart of the app. `CompositionRootProvider` builds it and gives it to the root, which builds `LocalProgressRepository` over it for the achievements screen (#188). `jest.setup.js` gives every test of the `app` project of Jest the official mock of the package.

`board/` holds the React Native board components (S3-07 in #151, Q8, issue #165). `BoardView` draws the pairs of a coordinate and a cell view of `GameViewModel` on any figure, with the accessible name of the board. `BoardLayout` is the screen layout of a figure, and `HexLayout`, `SquareLayout` and `TriangleLayout` implement it. `CellRenderer` is the interface of the cell renderer of a variant, and `SpaceCellRenderer` is the default renderer, which tells a free space from an occupied one (#170). `FitCellRenderer` draws a space of Hexa Fit: a socket, or a stone in the color of its piece (#175). `BoardView` hands a press on a space to the move controls of the game (#175). `fittedCellSize()` gives the widest cell at which a board fits the width of the screen.

`ui/` holds the parts that the screens share (#169): the design tokens of the team prototype in `theme.ts`, the scene of sky and grass, the hex stone, the wood sign and the lip button, and `useHomePresenter()`, which builds `HomePresenter` from the game registry of the root. `GameStage` is the view of the game screen: it keeps `GamePresenter`, starts the game and shows the board, the score and the outcome. `useGameSession()` builds the presenter of one game from the use cases and the screen parts of the root (#170). `MoveControls` is the strategy of the move controls of one game: the stage gives it the board and `play()`, which calls `GamePresenter.onMove()`. `BoardOnlyControls` is the default, and `FitControls` play Hexa Fit with the tray, the rotate button and a press on a space (#175). `useAchievements()` builds `AchievementsPresenter` from the root and gives the view model of the achievements screen (#188).
