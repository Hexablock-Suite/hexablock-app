### Changed

- Hexa Sort breaks a tie by the coordinate key ([#56](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/56)). When two stacks have top runs of the same size, the stack whose coordinate key comes first wins. `SortRules` compares the topology keys in plain string order (code units, `<`), never with `localeCompare`, so the result is the same on every device and never depends on when a cell was filled or emptied. Before, the first stack in `board.cells()` order won, and a cleared cell kept its old place in that order (gap G108.3). The test that pinned that history-dependent tie is replaced by tests of the key order. `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` moves the Sort rules of #56 from «Still to define» to decision D21: the whole top run moves, the size of the top run decides, and a top run of 10 or more removes the whole stack and counts one goal. The rule R-engine-no-game-id in `AGENTS.md` now names every form that `test/gates/engine-game-id-gate.test.ts` catches: `if`, `switch`, ternary, the left operand of `&&`, `||` and `??`, and table lookups, in `.ts` and `.tsx` files of every engine subfolder, and variables that hold the game id. The README and the _Check_ of APP-NFR-08 say the same.

- `«Sprint N»` markers in the class and extensibility views take N from `docs/ROADMAP.md`: N is the sprint of the requirement that the planned class serves, and the `data-requirement` attribute of each marker names it. `GeometryFactory`, `HexGeometry`, `BoardBuilder`, `IVariantFactory` and the four variant factories show Sprint 3 (APP-FR-16), not Sprint 2. `SquareGeometry` shows Sprint 1 (APP-NFR-13), not Sprint 3. The legends of the eight views, `docs/architecture/extensibility.md` and the alt text of the engine map state the rule, and the eight PNG images are exported again. The gate `test/docs/sprint-markers.test.ts` fails when a marker names no requirement or a requirement of another sprint, or when a legend keeps the old wording ([#89](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/89)).
- `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` records two offline sync rules that the team closed on 2026-11-06. DS-11: when the app holds a bundled copy and a backend copy of one level, the copy with the higher `version` wins. DS-12: when one account sends two results for one level, the backend keeps the best one. APP-FR-13, APP-FR-15 and A6 point to them, and the check of APP-FR-15 now also loads the higher `version`.

### Changed

- `GameEngine.play()` closes the second review of the Sprint 1 engine. A rejected move always returns `playing`, even when its resolution carries a final outcome, and `Resolution.evaluated()` throws on a rejected resolution. The scoring policy works on a copy of the state, and a score that is not finite throws a `RangeError` before the commit. The engine computes the next phase before the commit, so an unknown outcome throws a `RangeError` and leaves the board, the score and the phase unchanged. `pause()` before `start()` throws, as `play()` does. Tests pin that a failing publisher leaves the turn committed, that the rollback does not cover the piece source the rules hold, and that a victory publishes `GameOver` with `victory`.
- The board and the value objects reject input that the second review found. `Board.line()` from a coordinate off the board yields nothing. The `Board` constructor throws a `RangeError` for initial content off the shape or with a key that does not match its coordinate, and it copies each entry. `ProgressRecord.of()` throws for a score or stars that are not finite. `Radius`, `RegionSize` and `Turns` take safe integers only and store `-0` as `0`. `ShapeGroup` freezes its copy of the children. Tests pin the one-shot iterables of the board and `validMoves()`, and a seeded source that continues its sequence across two loops.
