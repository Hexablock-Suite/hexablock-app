# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository are documented in this file. The format follows [Keep a Changelog 1.1.0](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning 2.0.0](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

Each sprint of [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](docs/ROADMAP.md) closes with a version section and a `v0.N.0` tag (rule R-sprint-release in `AGENTS.md`). The final delivery is `v1.0.0`.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `docs/DEPENDENCIES.md` gives one reason for each entry of `dependencies` and `devDependencies` in `package.json`, and the gate `test/gates/dependencies-gate.test.ts` fails when `package.json` lists a package with no reason ([#186](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/186), rule R-dependencies-doc). An explanation is a list line that starts with the backticked name and gives a non-empty reason; the check is one-way, like the gate of the API. Seven packages of the Expo template that the app does not import say so in their line. The gate reads files only: it installs, removes and changes no dependency.
- Level selector of Hexa Fit and Hexa Sort ([#173](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/173)). The map opens `app/levels/[gameId].tsx` for a game with levels; it shows each level of the chosen figure as completed (with its stars), open or locked, from the `LevelStatus` that `ListLevels` gives, and an unlocked level starts by its id. A figure with no levels shows the text `levels.none`. `LevelSelectorPresenter` builds the view model, the composition root builds `ListLevels` once with `decorate()` over the one `LocalProgressRepository` and gives it through `listLevels()`, and `RegisteredGame.hasLevels` lets the map choose the route. Hexa Lines and Hexa Merge keep the direct route. Every text comes from the es/en catalog. Decision 63, four views and `classes.drawio` describe it.
- `LoadProgress` reads the saved progress of one game ([#181](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/181), S3-16 in #151, APP-FR-04). The use case takes the `ProgressRepository` port and the `GameId` of the game in its constructor, its request has no member, and `execute({})` answers the saved `GameProgress` as it is, or `undefined` when the game has none. A port that throws gives the new `load-failed` failure (classes.md decisions 18, 57 and 62). The composition root answers `loadProgress(gameId)` with one `LoadProgress` per game over its one `LocalProgressRepository`, and a root with no store throws a `RangeError`, as `listAchievements()` does.
- The game selector map shows the saved high score of each game ([#181](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/181), APP-FR-04). `HomePresenter` takes the saved progress of each game, and the choice of a game whose progress keeps a high score carries the record text composed from the new `map.highScore` catalog key and the score. `useSavedProgresses()` loads the progress of each registered game through the root, the map renders before the scores arrive and re-renders with them, and the signs of Hexa Lines and Hexa Merge show their record when the device keeps one. A failed load shows no score and no visible error: the `LoggingAspect` logs it.
- `docs/EXTENSIBILITY.md` records the extensibility rehearsal of 2026-11-25 ([#53](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/53), APP-NFR-06). The three jury variations, a new board size, a new scoring policy and a new piece shape, each sit on their own branch over `3ca471d`, and no diff touches `src/domain/engine/` or `src/application/`. The section lists what the rehearsal showed and how to repeat it before the defense.
- Fit and Sort levels on the square and on the triangle ([#354](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/354)). Each of the two figures has 3 Fit levels and 3 Sort levels, `fit-square-01` to `sort-triangle-03`, numbered from 1 on each figure, and the new Sort levels use a fixed list of stacks. The map shows Fit on every figure, and Fit starts a first level of three cells in a row there. APP-FR-16 no longer says that every bundled level is hex.
- `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` §12 classifies each business rule of the app as enterprise, application or mixed, with who defines it, how often it changes, where it lives in the code today and where it must live ([#339](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/339)). The rows that are not in place yet name the issue that moves or builds them: #277, #45 and #46.
- Play Hexa Lines on the shared game screen ([#174](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/174)). Pick a piece from the tray and tap the board to place it. A rejected placement keeps the selection. The tray refills after the last piece is placed, and the screen offers no undo or redo.

- Play Hexa Sort on the shared game screen ([#176](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/176)). The screen shows the offered stack and the level goal. Press a free space to place the stack. A cleared space can be used again. Sort counts each accepted placement once, keeps the count in its snapshot, and offers no undo or redo.

- The app consumes the API under the URI prefix `/v1` ([#346](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/346), paired with hexablock-api#173): `createApiClient().getHealth()` calls `/v1/health`, the consumer pact interaction and the CI contract job ask the `/v1` routes, `src/infrastructure/api/schema.d.ts` is regenerated against the paired API revision, and D28 of `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` mirrors API-NFR-15 of the API.
- The engine records the events of a move, `LinesCleared`, `TileMerged` and `StackCleared`, and `ApplyMove` delivers them before `GameOver` ([#361](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/361), classes.md decision 26). Each variant photo gives the events of its last move through `eventsOfLastMove()`, and the root builds each event with its `GameId` and `MatchId`. An undo keeps the events, so a counter keeps a move that the player later undid. Views `engine-1-turn`, `flow-1-apply-move`, `uc-2-game`, `variant-1-lines`, `variant-3-sort` and `variant-4-merge` show the events, and their PNG images are exported again.
- `GameEventBus` in layer 3, with `GameEventListener` and its first listener, `AchievementCounterListener` ([#362](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/362), classes.md decision 29). The bus gives each event to every listener in the order of subscription. A listener that throws does not stop the others: the bus then throws one error with the failures as its cause, and `ApplyMove` counts it as one failed delivery. `AchievementCounterListener` counts `LinesCleared`, `TileMerged` and `StackCleared` per game in memory, and #363 saves the counts. The composition root gives one bus to every `ApplyMove` in place of `NullGameEventPublisher`. Views `ad-3-events`, `ad-4-wiring` and the maps show the bus, and their PNG images are exported again.
- The counters of the achievements are saved on the device ([#363](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/363), part 3 of #277, classes.md decisions 57 and 60). `AchievementCounters`, an aggregate of layer 1 with the value object `AchievementCount`, keeps a best count and a total of each game. `AchievementCounterListener` saves the most lines at once and the highest tile as the best count, and adds each Sort goal to the total, through the new `AchievementCounterRepository` port. `LocalAchievementCounterRepository` saves them with a `version` under `achievement-counters:<game id>`, so the saved progress keeps its shape, and a text that it cannot read throws a `RangeError` and stays in the store. Views `uc-3-progress`, `ad-2-repositories`, `ad-4-wiring` and the maps show the change, and their PNG images are exported again.
- The four achievements of the move events complete from the saved counters ([#364](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/364), part 4 of #277, classes.md decisions 57, 58 and 60): clear 3 lines at once, reach 256 and reach 2048 in Hexa Merge, and complete 10 goals in Hexa Sort. Two goals of layer 1, `BestCountReached` and `TotalCountReached`, read the `AchievementCounters` of their game, and `ListAchievements` loads the counters of each game once through `AchievementCounterRepository`. A completed achievement stays completed after a restart. `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` says where the goal of each achievement lives. Views `uc-6-achievements`, `map-3-usecases` and `ad-4-wiring` show the change, and their PNG images are exported again.

### Fixed

- The layer texts name the four use cases that `GamePresenter` calls, `StartGame`, `ApplyMove`, `UndoMove` and `RedoMove`, in `layers.md`, the README and `src/infrastructure/README.md` ([#390](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/390), a part that an audit of #396 found open).
- A failed save of the achievement counters is reported ([#384](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/384)). `AchievementCounterListener` takes the `Logger` port of layer 2, and each failed save writes one `failure` entry with the code `save-failed` right away; the list `failures`, which grew with each failed event and which nobody read, is gone, and `saved()` only waits for the save queue. A logger that throws does not stop the later saves. The root gives the listener the logger of its drivers. Decisions 37 and 57, the READMEs, view `ad-3-events` and `classes.drawio` follow.
- On the triangle, a Fit piece lands on the pressed space ([#365](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/365), APP-FR-16, classes.md decision 14). `BoardFigure` gets the optional step `fitPlacementAt(at, rotation)`, next to `linesPlacementAt`, and the triangle gives `triangleFitPlacementAt`: the lattice cell of the pressed space minus the lattice cell of the turned origin. `FitControls` asks the step, so the turned origin of the piece goes on the pressed space at every rotation step and in every order of the pieces. The hexagon and the square place a piece as before. View `ad-4-wiring` shows the step, and its PNG image is exported again. A person still has to play the triangle levels on a screen.
- `GameProgress` refuses a progress record of another game ([#372](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/372), APP-FR-09, classes.md decision 56). `recordScore()` and `recordLevel()` ask the record `isOfGame()` and throw a `RangeError` for a record of another game, and the progress stays as it was. `SaveProgress` turns that error into a `save-failed` failure before it saves anything. View `engine-4-sources` shows the new query, and its PNG image is exported again.
- The determinism lint rule now rejects `performance.now()`, `crypto.randomUUID()`, `crypto.getRandomValues()` and `Date()` in domain and application code ([#324](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/324)).
- Keep Hexa Lines playing on a triangular board when a piece still fits. The board finds candidate placements by aligning a piece cell with a free space through the topology, rather than treating a triangle coordinate as a move offset.

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added

- The web build works ([#287](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/287)), a tool for the team and not a deliverable. `pnpm web:build` runs `expo export --platform web`, and `app.json` sets `web.output` to `static`, so each route is a static page that the browser hydrates. The game route lists one page per registered game with `generateStaticParams()`, so a reload of a game screen finds its page. On a wide window the content of each screen stays in a centered column of at most 560 points (`CONTENT_WIDTH`), and the figure selector stays centered. AsyncStorage keeps its values in `localStorage` on the web, so `DeviceKeyValueStore` needs no second adapter. `test/gates/platform-gate.test.ts` fails when code of `src/` outside `src/infrastructure/` reads the platform (rule R-platform-layer). `docs/web/` holds a screenshot of each screen at a phone and a desktop width, what works on the web and what does not, and the differences with the team prototype. Not seen on a phone.
- Slide the tiles of Hexa Merge with a swipe on the board, and with the keyboard on the web ([#292](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/292), classes.md decision 14). A swipe plays the way of the figure whose angle is nearest to its own, and a swipe shorter than 24 px plays nothing. On the web the arrow keys and the keys Q, E, Z and C play the way nearest to the angle of the key, and a repeated key plays once. A press that starts on a slide button or on the undo button stays a press, and the ring of slide buttons stays for the screen reader. `app/_layout.tsx` wraps the app in `GestureHandlerRootView` of `react-native-gesture-handler`, already a dependency. View 4.1 shows the change, and its PNG image is exported again.
- The hand-made levels of APP-FR-05 ([#49](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/issues/49), A5): `src/adapters/levels/fit/` now holds 15 Fit levels and `src/adapters/levels/sort/` holds 10 Sort levels, with rising difficulty. The new Fit levels `fit-04` to `fit-15` are `hex`, grow from 12 cells and 4 pieces to 36 cells and 8 pieces, and each one carries a witness that wins it. The new Sort levels `sort-04` to `sort-10` give a fixed list of stacks and grow from 2 goals in 5 moves to 4 goals in 10 moves. `BundledLevelRepository` imports every file. The count tests now need 15 Fit and 10 Sort level files, a test wins every Fit level with its witness through the real Fit rules, and a test wins every fixed Sort level by its plan with every star.
- A bug report issue form ([#317](https://github.com/Hexablock-Suite/hexablock-app/pull/317)): `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug-report.yml` asks for what happened, the steps to reproduce, the expected behavior, the platform and the screen, and `config.yml` keeps blank issues on.
